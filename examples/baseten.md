## MVP idea

_A proposal of what to build. Nothing has been built._

- **Goal:** Let an engineering team run a model endpoint through a launch-style traffic test before going live and see where it would break.
- **Target user:** Engineering and machine learning teams at AI-native startups putting models into production.
- **Core job:** Slow, fragile moves of models from prototype to production that break under real traffic.

**Must-have features**

- **Endpoint intake:** Lets a team point the check at one model endpoint it already has, the smallest step toward a launch test. `e3, e2`
- **Launch-style traffic test:** Directly tests the stated failure mode, infrastructure that breaks when real traffic arrives. `e1`
- **Failure report:** Shows where the endpoint would fail under load so the team can act before launch; the value must be validated in interviews. `e1, r16`
- **Pass/fail readiness summary:** Gives a single go or no-go view that a launch owner can share, tied to the production framing. `r16, e2`

**Out of scope**

- Pricing, billing, and plan tiers; marketing campaigns
- Model training and the Training product
- Agent sandboxes and Grid cost controls
- Multiple endpoint types or providers; automated endpoint fixes

**Success criteria**

- Interviewed teams confirm launch delay or traffic failure is a top-two blocker (action a5).
- At least one team agrees to run the check on a real endpoint in the concept test (action a3).
- The report identifies the failure point on a test endpoint in a form the team can act on.
- The check runs end to end on one endpoint type only.

**How a user tries it.** A platform engineer at an AI-native startup has a model endpoint due to launch next week. They point the readiness check at the endpoint, choose a launch-style traffic profile, and run it. When the run finishes, the report shows whether the endpoint held up and where it began to fail, with a single pass or fail summary to share with the team. The engineer then decides whether to fix the bottleneck before launching.
